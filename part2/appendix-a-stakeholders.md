# Appendix A: Stakeholder summary

**Table A1: stakeholders, the features that affect them and the evidence**

| Stakeholder | Type | Issues | How they are affected | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prospective borrowers (before sign-up) | Direct | A, B, D | Asked for location twice before any account; a second denial is permanent; cannot check the licence or ask a question in the app without a WhatsApp number | Figures 1–4, 6; Android Developers (2026) |
| Borrowers (after sign-up) | Direct | C, D | See a daily rate, not an APR, in the app; terms differ by channel; device data used for scoring | Figure 5; Credit Xpress (2026a, 2026c); store listings |
| People without WhatsApp | Direct (excluded) | D | No route to sign in or contact support | Figure 4 |
| Opg Capital Holdings | Operator | A–D | Gains scoring data and conversions; carries licence and store-policy risk | Licence WL3337; removed Play listing |
| The app's developers | Operator's workers | A–D | Build the prompts, screens and policy text; may not control the decisions | Figures 1–9; Figure 8 wording |
| Collection and support staff | Workers / partners | C, D | Act on contact data; support reachable only after sign-in | Credit Xpress (2026b) |
| Google, Apple, Meta (WhatsApp) | Business partners | A, B, C, D | Host the app or carry its sign-in; their policies are what the app is measured against | Google (2026); Apple (2026) |
| Credit reporting agencies, banks, e-wallets | Business partners | C | Receive or move borrower data and money | Credit Xpress (2026b) |
| Emergency contacts and address-book contacts | Indirect | D | Data collected without their knowledge; may be contacted about someone else's debt | Figure 9; Credit Xpress (2026a); store data declarations |
| Borrowers' households | Indirect | C | Share the cost of a debt that was hard to price | Credit Xpress (2026c) |
| KPKT and the licensed-lending market | Indirect | C, D | Regulator's cap is the main price safeguard; trust in licensed apps depends on them looking different from illegal ones | The Vibes (2025) |
